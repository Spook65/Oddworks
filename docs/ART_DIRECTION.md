# ODDWORKS Art Direction

## Visual Identity

ODDWORKS uses industrial toybox absurdism.

The world should feel like a colorful workshop made from practical parts, toy-like machinery, weird salvage, and expressive handmade creatures. It should be funny and strange without becoming visually noisy or hard to read.

## Core Traits

- colorful
- chunky
- clean silhouettes
- exaggerated proportions
- humorous
- readable at Roblox camera distances
- weird but friendly
- expressive
- intentionally original

Oddlings should ideally be recognizable from silhouette. A player should be able to glance across a workshop and identify the important creatures, stations, upgrades, and interactable objects.

## Oddling Design Language

Oddlings are original absurd collectible characters. They can combine familiar object categories with creature logic, workshop materials, and theatrical personality.

Examples of the creative language include:

- armored toast
- traffic-cone wizard
- tiny forklift creature
- absurd appliance creature
- office-object monster

These are examples of design language, not locked final content.

## World Design Language

The workshop and junkyard should favor:

- blocky readable machinery
- bold material contrast
- clear interactable shapes
- playful industrial props
- visible upgrade stages
- display areas that make collections feel physical
- compact spaces that support social viewing

Interactive objects should be legible before they are beautiful. Decorative clutter must not hide gameplay.

## Originality Rules

Do not use or create:

- direct copies of existing brainrot characters
- copyrighted characters
- trademark or logo copying
- internet celebrity likenesses
- near-identical parody models
- ripped models or assets from another Roblox game
- thumbnails, UI, or art copied wholesale from another game

Absurdity is the inspiration category. Existing meme IP is not the asset library.

ODDWORKS should aim to create original characters capable of becoming memes rather than copying memes that already exist.

## Asset Use

Third-party assets may be considered only when their rights and permissions are clear and documented. Do not assume an asset is safe because it is available in the Roblox ecosystem or used by another experience.

When in doubt, build original placeholder assets first and replace them later through a documented art pass.
